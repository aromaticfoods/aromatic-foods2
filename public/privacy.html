Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Aromatic Foods ("we", "us") sells homemade pickles (achar) and fruit preserves (murabba) from Multan, Pakistan. This page explains what information we collect when you visit our website or place an order, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, city, delivery address, the items you order and any notes you add.
  • Website usage data: basic information about how you use the site (pages viewed, items added to cart), collected through cookies and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel.

How we use it

  • To confirm, prepare and deliver your order, and to contact you about it (usually on WhatsApp or by phone).
  • To keep a record of our orders.
  • To measure and improve our advertising on Facebook and Instagram.

Who can see your information

Your order details are recorded in our private Google Sheet, which only we can access, and are sent to us on WhatsApp. We share your name, phone number and address with our courier partners (such as TCS or Leopards) only so they can deliver your parcel. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and Meta Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, to understand which of our ads lead to visits and orders, and to show our ads to people who may be interested. The Pixel uses cookies and may share limited usage data with Meta. You can control ad personalisation in your Facebook/Instagram ad settings, or block cookies in your browser settings.

Data retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to run our business and meet our obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us on WhatsApp or through the details on our Contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
